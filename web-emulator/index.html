Assemble the kit

  1. Seat the e-paper FPC in the 24-pin connector, metal side up, and close the latch.
  2. Set the display jumper to 24Pin–GND.
  3. Connect the battery to the JST header (red = +, black = −).
  4. Plug the XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus into the driver board and connect USB-C.
  5. Plug into your laptop and ask Codex "set up my Dev Day terminal". It reads the Codex install already signed in on your machine and pushes your pet, usage and weather over USB — no account, no permission prompt. After that, plugging in refreshes on its own.

Make it yours

Nothing here is locked. Swap the pet:

tools/dash_sync.py --pet dewey            # a built-in companion
tools/dash_sync.py --pet ~/.codex/pets/mypet

Redraw a page in firmware/cards.cpp and preview it without a device (tools/preview/build.sh), or replace the factory app outright — raw USB flashing is unrestricted:

arduino-cli compile -b esp32:esp32:XIAO_ESP32S3_Plus MyApp
arduino-cli upload  -b esp32:esp32:XIAO_ESP32S3_Plus -p PORT MyApp
